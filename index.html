Evolution and AI

Related proteins keep the same fold, and residues that touch tend to mutate together. A multiple sequence alignment (MSA) of homologs carries that signal; mean-field direct coupling analysis (DCA) pulls it out, and a residual neural network turns it into a distance histogram for every residue pair plus a secondary-structure call. Those predictions are what “MSA + AI” folds with.
