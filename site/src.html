    How the fold is found

    The lab never sees the answer. It starts from the sequence and the protein's evolutionary relatives, and only compares with the PDB structure at the end.

    Ten proteins, three seeds each

    Distance from the real structure after folding with v8, averaged over three random seeds of 12 replicas. Lower is better; under about 3 Å the fold is essentially right.

    TM-score runs from 0 to 1, and above 0.5 usually means the same fold. It is harsh on very small peptides such as chignolin and Trp-cage, which can sit close in Å and still score low.
